# Context at runtime boundaries

Restore project/milestone/task policy Documents on resume and pass the refreshed inventory through
`--context-policy`. Task overrides milestone, then project, then built-in defaults. Token overrides
never lower higher defaults or change counts, bytes, attempts, cumulative budgets, or approvals.

Before dispatch compile and validate the complete packet, including instructions, skills, plan,
evidence and policy/runtime bindings. Verify the role handoff against its packet hash and measured
size. Label UTF-8/4 estimates separately from native telemetry. Recompile after policy/model/harness
changes; reject known input-capacity overflow after reservations. Report unknown capacity.

Record new attempts as evidenced delta Documents and link them from verification comments. Keep
history out of active packets. After two identical failures without acceptance improvement, diagnose once; replan only for material drift; environment failures require prerequisite recovery. Four automatic attempts remain the
upper bound. Stronger execution, integration, and lifecycle gates retain their approvals.

At durable boundaries suggest compaction at the lesser of 70% of the effective budget and 21,000
tokens, over 4,000 history tokens, or after three repair/handoff events. Compaction targets are
capped absolutely so a raised ceiling never inflates durable snapshots. A declined unchanged suggestion remains inactive. To prepare
or activate a checkpoint, load [context management](context-management.md): exact preview approval,
fresh sources, snapshot read-back, then active-reference update. Never increase a budget implicitly.
